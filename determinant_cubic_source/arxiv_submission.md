## Abstract (≤ 250 words)

We consider the gradient flow $\dot\Gamma = -\nabla P(\Gamma;\mu,J)$ on the real $4\times4$ matrices,
with potential $P = \|\Gamma\|^2 + \mu\det\Gamma + \beta\|\Gamma\|^4 + b_6\|\Gamma\|^6 - \langle J,\Gamma\rangle$,
and its damped second-order version. The field-free potential is invariant under the bilateral orthogonal
action $\Gamma \mapsto U\Gamma V^\top$ with $\det U \det V = 1$.

The ring of polynomial invariants of this action is generated by the symmetric functions of the singular
values and by the determinant; the latter is the only orientation-sensitive generator. The naive candidate
for a cubic invariant, $\operatorname{tr}\Gamma^3$, lies outside the bilateral ring: under $(U,V)\in G$,
$\operatorname{tr}(U\Gamma V^\top)^3$ depends on $V^\top U$ and reduces to $\operatorname{tr}\Gamma^3$
only when $V=U$.

Near a Hessian degeneracy with a simple soft mode, centre-manifold (Lyapunov–Schmidt) reduction yields
the local normal forms. The cubic coefficient receives from the determinant, through its cofactor matrix,
its unique orientation-sensitive contribution. An explicit Taylor expansion of $\det(\Gamma_*+\xi V)$
shows that $D^3\!\det(\Gamma_*)[\cdot]$ is linear in the equilibrium $\Gamma_*$ and that the soft-mode
cubic is the leading orientation-sensitive term on the stratum $V\perp\Gamma_*$.

We prove the codimension-1 reduction (fold and pitchfork) with explicit coefficients, and the
codimension-2 cusp $A_3$. A no-Hopf lemma shows that no metric-gradient flow, for any positive-definite
metric, admits an oscillatory bifurcation: the Jacobian has real spectrum. The same mechanism, under
conjugation symmetry $O(n)$, selects $\operatorname{tr}Q^3$ as in the Landau–de Gennes energy of the
nematic $Q$-tensor. Each statement is accompanied by a reproducible numerical verification.
