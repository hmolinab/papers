# The determinant as the source of the cubic term

Normal-form reduction in a matrix gradient flow.

**Author:** Henry Molina · hmolinab@unal.edu.co
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.20752208](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20752208)

Near a simple degeneration of the Hessian of the gradient flow
\(\dot\Gamma=-\nabla P\), \(P=\|\Gamma\|^2+\mu\det\Gamma+\beta\|\Gamma\|^4\) on the real 4×4
matrices, center-manifold reduction yields the local normal forms of bifurcation theory, and the
cubic coefficient comes from the determinant (through its cofactor matrix). The paper classifies the
fold, pitchfork, cusp and Bogdanov–Takens organizing centers, and states a conjecture for the
non-gradient (Shilnikov) chaos.
